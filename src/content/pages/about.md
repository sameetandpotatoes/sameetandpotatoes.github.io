Sameet Sapra is a Software Engineer at Meta, based in Chicago. He holds a B.S. and M.C.S. in Computer Science (with a minor in Mathematical Statistics) from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Class of 2019.

## Now

Building software and following what's new in AI. He lives in Chicago's West Loop. This site collects his writing and published work.

## Background

At UIUC, Sameet worked on Project Groot, ACM at UIUC's microservice-based rebuild of its web infrastructure, and wrote about that work in "SOA Made Simple" (March 30, 2017). His earlier writing here covers internships, open source, and lessons from getting hacked in 2023.
