Sameet Sapra is a Software Engineer at Meta, based in Chicago. He holds a B.S. and M.C.S. in Computer Science (with a minor in Mathematical Statistics) from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Class of 2019.

## Publications

- Co-author, "Scalable data classification for security and privacy," *Engineering at Meta*, July 21, 2020, with Nikolay Pavlovich Laptev and Paulo Tanaka: https://engineering.fb.com/2020/07/21/security/data-classification-system/
- Co-author, "Scalable Data Classification for Security and Privacy," arXiv:2006.14109 (2020), with Paulo Tanaka and Nikolay Laptev: https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2006.14109
- Co-author, Groot poster for Engineering Open House 2017, ACM at UIUC, presented March 10–11, 2017: https://github.com/acm-uiuc/publications/blob/HEAD/Groot_EOH2017_Poster/README.md
- Co-author, "Data-Driven Approach to Picking the Most Optimal Classification Algorithm," Illinois Data Science Initiative technical report, June 26, 2017.

At UIUC, Sameet worked on Project Groot, ACM at UIUC's microservice-based rebuild of its web infrastructure, and wrote about that work in "SOA Made Simple" (March 30, 2017).
